\chapter{Evidence Manifest}\label{app:manifest}
This appendix lists the provenance of the two full-size secure runs, Secure \ac{bp} (Table~\ref{tab:app-manifest}) and Secure \ac{ff} (Table~\ref{tab:app-manifest-ff}), so that the runs can be checked and, in a separate controlled environment, repeated. Paths beginning with \texttt{\textasciitilde/} are on the execution host and are not part of the repository; repository paths are relative to its root. Secret-bearing material (MP-SPDZ \texttt{Player-Data}, party keys and certificates, the raw party input files) is deliberately excluded from the thesis package. All hashes are SHA-256. The Secure \ac{bp} hashes were recomputed for this thesis on 16 November 2026; the Secure \ac{ff} hashes are those recorded by the run and by the evidence files of the research repository, and the hashes of the saved run artifacts were read on 24 November 2026. No MPC program was executed to produce this appendix.
